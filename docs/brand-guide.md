## About us

BeaverBids watches every federal and provincial procurement portal overnight: CanadaBuys, MERX, BC Bid, SEAO, NBON and the rest. Firms get only the tenders that fit their trade, their province and their bonding capacity, in their inbox by Monday, 7 a.m.

Each opportunity comes with a plain language summary of scope, deadlines and mandatory criteria, plus a first draft proposal. It is built for the shop, not the enterprise: trades and small businesses of 5 to 100 people with no bid team, certified Indigenous, women and veteran owned suppliers, and the consultants and bid writers who work for them.

| Figure | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| 8+ | Portals to check by hand, every single day |
| 70% | Of relevant tenders never seen in time |
| 3 days | Average lead time lost to manual searching |
| $0 | Spent on the contracts you never found |

**Voice.** Plain, direct and specific. We speak to the owner or estimator in the second person, promise less and show more, and never use hype.

Note: the portal list and the first draft proposal above are brand positioning. Phase 1 imports CanadaBuys only and ships no proposal writer; see docs/development-guide.md.

## Logo on backgrounds

The logo is the beaver mascot beside the BEAVERBIDS wordmark, set in Archivo Expanded ExtraBold.

| Background | Treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Paper #F3F1EC | Wordmark in asphalt, no keyline on the mascot |
| Concrete #CAC5BD | Wordmark in asphalt, no keyline on the mascot |
| Asphalt #23221F | Wordmark in paper, mascot takes a medium paper keyline (#F3F1EC) around its outline |
| Photography | Same as asphalt: paper keyline, over an asphalt shade |

**Rules**

- The mascot keeps its own colours on every background.
- On asphalt and over photography the mascot takes a paper keyline, the same colour as the wordmark there, so it lifts off the dark ground. No keyline on light grounds.
- The wordmark is asphalt on light grounds and paper on dark ones, and is never set in signal red.
- The mascot may stand alone as an icon, and the wordmark may stand alone where the mascot would be too small to read.

## Clear space and minimum size

**Clear space.** X is the cap height of BEAVERBIDS. Keep at least X clear on every side of the lockup. Nothing enters this space: no text, edges, photos or other logos.

**Proportions.** Scale the mascot and wordmark together. The mascot is centred on the wordmark, and the gap between them is a quarter of the mascot height.

| Minimum size | Rule |
| --- | --- |
| 32 px | Mascot height in the full lockup on screen. Below 24 px, drop the wordmark and use the mascot alone |
| 14 mm | Mascot height when it stands alone in print |

## Logo: correct use

| Use | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Full lockup on paper | The default for documents, the website and print |
| Full lockup on concrete | Hero areas, covers and calls to action |
| Paper keyline on asphalt | Dark sections, footers and business cards |
| Keyline over a shaded photo | Keep the asphalt shade (86 percent) under it as well as the keyline |

## Logo: incorrect use

- **Don't recolour it.** The mascot keeps its own colours and the wordmark stays asphalt or paper. Red is for actions, never the logo.
- **Don't stretch or squash it.** Always scale proportionally.
- **Don't rotate it.** The lockup always sits level.
- **Don't place it on a busy photo.** Without the asphalt shade the mascot and name break up.
- Redrawing the mascot or retyping the name in another font is also off limits.

## Colour palette

| Name | Hex | RGB | Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Signal red | #D2311F | 210 49 31 | The only accent. Buttons, one highlight per headline, live values and markers |
| Asphalt | #23221F | 35 34 31 | Text on light backgrounds and dark sections |
| Concrete | #CAC5BD | 202 197 189 | Covers, hero areas and calls to action |
| Paper | #F3F1EC | 243 241 236 | Reading pages and text on asphalt |
| Slab | #DEDAD3 | 222 218 211 | Cards on paper |
| Bone | #FAF9F6 | 250 249 246 | Forms and panels |

**Mix.** About 60 percent paper and concrete, 30 percent asphalt, under 10 percent red.

**Rules**

- Use white or paper text on signal red.
- Use red as text only at large sizes (24 px and up) on paper or asphalt, never on concrete.
- The beaver mascot is the one exception to this palette: it keeps its own browns and hard hat red wherever it appears.

## Typography

All three families are free under the SIL Open Font License from Google Fonts. Archivo Expanded is Archivo at its widest setting (width 125).

| Family | Use | Rules |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Archivo Expanded ExtraBold | Headlines, big numbers and the logo | Always uppercase. Tight leading, one red highlight per headline, never below 18 px |
| Archivo Regular and Bold | Body and titles | Regular in sentence case for body copy. Bold in uppercase for card titles and buttons |
| IBM Plex Mono Medium | Labels and data | Small labels in wide spaced capitals, and data lines like "RE: demos@beaverbids.ca" |

## Mood board

Five reference images set the tone, light and framing. New photography should sit comfortably beside them.

| Shot | Description |
| --- | --- |
| 01 | Estimator reading plans on a truck tailgate at golden hour |
| 02 | Small crew unloading materials in a gravel yard, overcast sky |
| 03 | Worn gloves on a site clipboard |
| 04 | Site trailer desk: plans, coffee, laptop |
| 05 | Concrete wall with hard shadow bars |

| Look for | Avoid |
| --- | --- |
| Real people doing real work on real sites | Office handshakes and posed smiles at screens |
| Low sun or flat overcast light | Spotless hard hats lined up in a row |
| Warm, slightly muted colour with deep shadows | Blue corporate colour grading |
| Room at the bottom of the frame for text | Flags or maple leaves as decoration |
